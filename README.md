# Hi, I'm Manikanta Chintapalli 👋

### Embedded Developer | Low-Level Systems Enthusiast | Building at the Silicon Layer

I am an Embedded Developer driven by a deep curiosity for what happens "under the hood." I don't just write code; I explore the register level, optimizing logic where every byte and clock cycle matters. I am passionate about bridging the gap between raw hardware signals and robust firmware architecture.

---

### 🚀 The Deep Dive

*   **Bare-Metal Foundations:** I focus on writing lean, efficient code in **C** and **Assembly** (8051/ARM). I enjoy the challenge of making every byte count in resource-constrained environments.
*   **Silicon-Level Interaction:** Actively building drivers and interfacing with **STM32**, **ARM**, and **8051** architectures. I prefer working directly with registers to understand how the hardware truly breathes.
*   **Protocol Implementation:** I enjoy bridging the gap between devices using wired protocols like **UART, I2C,** and **SPI**, while exploring the IoT space through **MQTT** and **BLE**.
*   **Signal-to-Logic Translation:** I use **oscilloscopes** and **logic analyzers** to decode RF signals (like EV1527) and transform raw waveforms into reliable firmware logic.
*   **Firmware Craftsmanship:** Beyond just "making it work," I prioritize architecture. I am currently focused on implementing robust data structures like **Circular Buffers** and **Linked Queues** to ensure system stability.
